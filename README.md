# 🪐 Reach the Planet

A fast, space-themed 2-player racing game that runs in your browser from a single HTML file. No install, no dependencies.

Two astronauts race down neon lanes toward a runaway planet. Dodge hazards, manage your stamina, and be the first to reach it. Win two rounds to take the series.

## ▶️ How to play

Open `reach_the_planet.html` in any modern browser, pick your characters and press **Play locally**.

| | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Jump | `W` | `↑` |
| Sprint | `D` | `→` |

`Space` pauses. On touchscreens, use the on-screen buttons.

## 🌌 Gameplay

- ☄️ **Asteroids** and 🕳️ **black holes** slow you down
- ⭐ **Stardust** gives a turbo boost and refills some stamina
- 🟢 **Launch pads** fling you high and give a speed boost
- ⚡ **Stamina:** sprinting drains the bar, and an empty bar means you are tired until it recovers
- 🏆 **Best of 3:** first to win 2 rounds takes the series
- ⏱️ Your best time is saved in your browser

Obstacles are shuffled each race, but both lanes get the same mix, so it stays fair.

## ✨ Features

- Animated starfield, nebulae, shooting stars and a ringed planet
- Particle effects, screen shake and jetpack flames
- Crazy winner phrases 🎉
- Sound effects, with a mute toggle
- Responsive layout, with touch controls for phones and tablets
- Online play when hosted on claude.ai (see the note below)

## 🌐 A note on online play

Online 2-player mode uses the room feature on claude.ai and only works on the published page. In a standalone copy from GitHub, the online section shows a message and local 2-player mode works as normal.

## 🛠️ Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript using the Canvas API. The whole game is one file.
